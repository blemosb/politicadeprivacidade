Privacy Policy - MemoBear

1. Photos and Images

Photos chosen by the user remain only on the device and are not uploaded to external servers.

2. Ads

MemoBear uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect anonymous usage data according to Google's own privacy policy.

3. In-app Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the official app store platform, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. MemoBear does not directly access the user's payment information.

4. World Leaderboard

MemoBear includes an optional world leaderboard feature. The app asks for the user's consent before uploading any score or profile information to the world leaderboard.

If the user agrees to participate, MemoBear may send the following information to our servers: player name, country, avatar, board or game mode, match time, number of moves, match date, and an anonymous user identifier.

This information may be visible to other players in the world leaderboard. If the user does not agree, no score or profile information is uploaded to the world leaderboard.

5. Cloud Services

MemoBear may use Firebase services to store and retrieve world leaderboard data, authenticate users anonymously, and support leaderboard functionality.
