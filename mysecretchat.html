Privacy Policy — My Secret Schat

Last updated: September 17, 2026

My Secret Schat ("the app") is a demonstration chat and file-sharing application published to showcase the developer's work. It is intended for evaluation by people interested in ordering a custom-built application, which is delivered privately as an APK file.

1. Data we process

  • Display name — the name you type in the app. It is stored on your device and attached to the messages you send so other participants can identify you.
  • Device identifier — an Android device ID used to register your license and to mark message delivery and read status.
  • Messages and media — text, photos, videos, audio and files you send are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage) so other participants can receive them.
  • Delivery and read status — timestamps recording when a message was received or viewed by each device.
  • Push notification token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to deliver notifications to your device.

2. What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No account credentials, email addresses or contact lists.
  • No location data.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for advertising purposes.

3. Service providers

The app relies on Google Firebase services (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Cloud Functions), operated by Google. Data may be processed on Google servers, which can be located outside your country. Google's processing is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

4. Security

All communication between the app and Firebase uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored in readable form in the database and can be accessed by the project administrator.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Messages remain stored until deleted. Any participant can delete their messages, and the app's emergency function removes all messages and their stored media from the conversation.
  • License records (device ID, display name and notification token) remain while your license is active.

6. Permissions

The app requests access to the camera, microphone, media library and notifications only to provide the features you trigger (sending photos, videos, audio and receiving notifications). Media is accessed only when you explicitly attach it to a message.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
