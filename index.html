Privacy Policy - GeoLink

GeoLink is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

  • Location Data: Used to display your position on the map and provide location-based features.
  • Device Information: Limited technical details such as operating system and app version, used to improve performance.
  • Notifications: If enabled, we may send push notifications related to app updates or important information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app’s core functionality.
  • To ensure app stability and fix technical issues.
  • To communicate important updates to users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only used internally to improve the app experience.

User Rights

  • You can disable location services at any time in your device settings.
  • You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at support@geolink.com.

Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
