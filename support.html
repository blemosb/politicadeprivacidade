GeoLink Support

GeoLink is a marketplace for buying and selling natural stones with an integrated inspection process. If you need help with your account, orders, inspections, or shipments, contact us using the information below.

Email: sanclergemstones@gmail.com

We usually respond within 2 business days.

What we can help with

  • Account access and login issues
  • Orders and order status
  • Inspection process and documentation
  • Shipment tracking
  • Technical problems with the app
  • Data deletion requests

Payments

GeoLink does not process payments inside the app. The inspection fee is paid by the buyer to GeoLink outside the app. The purchase of the stone is arranged separately between the buyer and the seller.
